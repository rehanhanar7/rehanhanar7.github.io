Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Rehan ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our services, including:

  • Android Apps Tamil Nadu Government App, Manapparai Info App, Words to Pages Converter App
  • Chrome Extension Rehan Job Helper

By using our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We encourage you to read this policy carefully to understand how your data is handled.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Android Applications

User-Provided Information: For the Manapparai Info App, users may voluntarily submit business information including business name, address, phone number, and business category. This information is:

  • Submitted voluntarily by users
  • Verified by us before being displayed in the app
  • Used solely to provide local business information to other users
  • Not collected automatically

Analytics Data: Our apps use Google Play Services and AdMob, which may collect:

  • Device information (model, operating system version)
  • App usage statistics
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

1.2 Chrome Extension (Rehan Job Helper)

Privacy-First Design: The Rehan Job Helper extension stores all user data locally on your device using Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local). We have no server. Nothing is collected or transmitted unless you use a feature that needs it, and AI features ask for your permission before any data is sent.

Data Stored Locally: The following data is stored locally in your browser and is never sent to our servers:

  • Profile (saved form fields): Personal information you save, which may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio URLs, salary information, notice period, work authorisation answers, date of birth, passport number, educational history, employment history, headline, any other answers you choose to save, and optional alternative wordings you add to a field
  • Resume file: A PDF or Word file (up to 5 MB) you add so it can be attached to resume upload fields
  • Remembered answers: For each website, answers you type or correct on application forms: the website name, the question's label, and either the answer or the name of the saved field it matches, so the same form fills automatically next time
  • Job Application Tracker Data: Company name, role name, role URL, location, application status, date added, and last updated timestamp for jobs you track or save from a job ad
  • Job Site Bookmarks: A list of job portal websites organized by country, including both default sites and any custom sites you add
  • AI Settings: Your OpenAI and/or Anthropic API key (kept on this device, or only until Chrome closes if you choose that option), chosen models, effort and output limits, which AI providers you have allowed, token usage counts, prompt templates, and resume text you enter for the Fit check
  • Extension Preferences: Enabled/disabled toggle, autofill options, input/output visibility toggle
  • Keyword Highlight Settings: Include/exclude keyword lists for job listing filtering (developer feature)

Where the extension runs: By default only on major job portals (for example Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, LinkedIn, Indeed, Naukri and SEEK). It runs on other websites only if you turn on "Use on all websites" in Settings, or when you press the fill shortcut on a page.

Data Accessed from Web Pages: When you click "Fill this page" or press the fill shortcut, the extension reads the form on that page:

  • Form field labels and attributes (name, id, placeholder, aria-label, type, required status) and nearby section headings
  • Dropdown and radio-button options, and values already in the fields (so it doesn't overwrite your answers)
  • While a form it has filled is open, answers you type into those fields, to remember them for that website (stored locally only)
  • Job ad details (title, company, location, description) when you save a job, check your fit, or draft AI answers on a page that contains a job posting
  • Field values when you click the "Save" button on a field

Passwords, search boxes and hidden fields are never read or filled, and consent or terms checkboxes are never ticked for you.

No Chrome Sync: This extension uses only chrome.storage.local (and chrome.storage.session for an API key you choose to keep only until Chrome closes). Your data does not sync across devices via your Google account.

Data Sent to Third Parties: When you use the optional AI features (AI answers and Fit check), certain data is sent to the AI provider you choose, OpenAI or Anthropic, after you allow it. See Section 3.2 for full details.

2. How We Use Information

2.1 Android Applications

  • To provide and maintain app functionality
  • To improve user experience and app features
  • To display relevant business information (Manapparai Info App)
  • To analyze app usage and fix bugs
  • To display advertisements through AdMob

2.2 Chrome Extension

  • Form Autofill: To fill job application forms from your saved profile when you click "Fill this page" or press the fill shortcut. This works entirely in your browser, without AI.
  • Remembered answers: To fill the same form on the same website automatically next time, using answers you typed or corrected
  • Job Application Tracking: To help you track and manage job applications locally on your device
  • Job Site Management: To provide a curated list of job portal websites and allow you to manage custom bookmarks
  • AI answers (Optional): When you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key and click "Draft answers with AI", the questions your profile couldn't answer, your saved profile values (up to 500 characters each), the resume text you selected in Fit check (if any) and the job ad (when the page has one) are sent to your chosen provider to draft answers
  • Fit check (Optional): Your resume text, your resume file if you tick "Send my resume file", the job description and the scoring prompt are sent to your chosen provider to generate a fit score and analysis
  • Clipboard Operations: To copy saved field values or AI-generated reports to your clipboard when you click a copy button
  • Data Import/Export: To export your data as a JSON backup file (including remembered answers and your resume file) or import a previous backup. API keys are included only if you confirm when exporting.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Android Applications

Our apps use the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google Play Services - For app functionality and analytics
  • AdMob - For displaying advertisements

3.2 Chrome Extension

The Rehan Job Helper extension communicates with the following third-party services only when you use specific features:

a) OpenAI API or Anthropic API (Optional — requires your own API key)

If you use the AI features, the extension sends data directly from your browser to the provider you choose in Settings: OpenAI or Anthropic. The first time you use an AI feature with a provider, the extension asks for your permission and sends nothing until you allow it. You can stop sharing at any time in Settings.

  • AI answers — data sent:
    • The questions on the current form that your profile couldn't answer (labels, types, required status, section headings and options)
    • Your saved profile values (labels and values, each up to 500 characters). This may include personal information such as your name, email, phone number, address, salary, employment history and other data you have saved.
    • The resume text you selected in Fit check, if any
    • The job ad on the page (title, company, description), when the page contains a job posting or you captured it
  • Fit check — data sent:
    • The resume text you enter or select, and your resume file if you tick "Send my resume file"
    • The job description text
    • The prompt template used for analysis
  • Test key: Checking your API key requests only the provider's list of available models. No profile data is sent.

Requests to OpenAI are made with response storage turned off. Each provider processes data under its own terms: OpenAI's Privacy Policy and Terms of Use, and Anthropic's Privacy Policy and Commercial Terms. We do not control how these providers handle data sent via their APIs. You use these features at your own discretion, with your own API key, and usage is billed to your provider account.

b) GitHub Pages (Static Data Fetch)

The extension may fetch the default list of job portal websites and default AI prompts from https://rehanhanar7.github.io (hosted on GitHub Pages). This is a read-only download of data — no user data is sent to this server.

No other third-party services: The extension does not use any analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry services.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Android Applications

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4.2 Chrome Extension

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. We implement:

  • Local-only storage: All user data is stored on your device. We do not operate any cloud servers or databases for this extension.
  • Chrome's built-in security: Data is protected by Chrome's security mechanisms and your operating system's user account protections
  • No remote backups: We do not create or maintain any copies of your data on external servers
  • Import/export for portability: You can export your data as a JSON file and import it on another device
  • Full user control: You can view, edit, and delete all stored data at any time through the extension's interface
  • API key storage: If you use AI features, your OpenAI or Anthropic API key is stored in plain text in Chrome's local storage on your device, or in Chrome's session storage (cleared when Chrome closes) if you untick "Keep the key on this device". We recommend keeping your API key confidential and not sharing exported files that contain it.
  • Resume file: Your resume file is stored locally and is only attached to upload fields on forms you fill, or sent to your AI provider if you choose that in Fit check.

4.3 Data Retention

Chrome Extension: Your data is retained in Chrome's local storage indefinitely until you choose to delete it. Data is automatically removed when you uninstall the extension. There is no server-side data retention since we do not store any data on our servers.

Android Applications: Data collected through third-party services (Google Play Services, AdMob) is retained according to their respective privacy policies. Business information submitted to the Manapparai Info App is retained until you request its deletion.

5. Chrome Extension Permissions

The Rehan Job Helper extension requests the following permissions, each with a specific and limited purpose:

5.1 Storage Permission

Purpose: To store user-saved form data, job application details, job site bookmarks, AI configuration settings, and extension preferences locally in the browser using chrome.storage.local.

Data Scope: Only data you explicitly save or configure is stored. No data is collected automatically.

5.2 Active Tab Permission

Purpose: Lets the fill keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+F) fill the page you are on, one time, even on websites the extension doesn't otherwise have access to.

Data Scope: Access is limited to the active tab and only when you press the shortcut.

5.3 Side Panel Permission

Purpose: To display the extension's interface for autofill, your profile, job applications, job sites, Fit check and settings.

Data Scope: Interface only, no additional data access beyond what is stored locally.

5.4 Scripting Permission

Purpose: To add the autofill tools to a page (including embedded application forms) when you click "Fill this page" or press the shortcut, and to show the save/fill buttons on form fields.

Data Scope: The tools only read and fill form fields and job ad details on that page, as described in Section 1.2.

5.5 Clipboard Write Permission

Purpose: To copy saved field values or evaluation reports to your clipboard when you click a copy button, including when a page won't accept autofill.

Data Scope: Only the specific value you choose to copy is written to the clipboard. No data is read from the clipboard.

5.6 Host Permissions (job portals, AI providers, job-site data)

Purpose: Access to major job portals (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, Jobvite, Workable, BambooHR, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Breezy, Taleo, SuccessFactors, LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, SEEK and Bayt) so autofill and the field buttons work there; access to api.openai.com and api.anthropic.com for the optional AI features you use; and access to rehanhanar7.github.io to download the job-site list.

Data Scope: The field buttons appear on those portals, and forms are only read or filled when you ask. The AI addresses receive data only when you use an AI feature you have allowed.

5.7 Optional Access to All Websites

Purpose: Job applications are hosted on thousands of company websites. If you turn on "Use on all websites" in Settings, the extension can fill forms and show its field buttons on any website. It is off by default, Chrome asks you to confirm it, and you can turn it off at any time.

Data Scope: Same as the job portals: forms are only read or filled when you ask. No background data collection occurs.
