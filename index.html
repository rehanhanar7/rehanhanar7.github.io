I’m Rehan. I architect and deliver large-scale enterprise web, mobile and AI platforms, and I lead the global teams that build them.

Most of my recent work sits where applied generative AI meets real enterprise constraints: retrieval over locked-down document estates, LLM workflow automation with PII controls, and agentic pipelines that quietly remove hours from a sprint.

Alongside it I build foundations: a centralised UI platform serving 70+ UPS applications, a cybersecurity ticketing platform for GE, a configuration-driven insurance UI that pulled a red-status programme back to renewal, and a government mobile app I supported on the ground at Cairo ICT.

I’ve led teams of 8 to 30 for clients across the US, Canada, Europe, Singapore, Malaysia, Egypt and Saudi Arabia. The through-line is the same: shorten the distance between a decision and a shipped thing.

Outside work I build and run my own products: a Quran study site, a bilingual guide to my home town, and a Chrome extension for job seekers. See the hobby builds.
